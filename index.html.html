Credits, explained

Credits pay for seconds of new AI video.

1 credit costs roughly 6–8¢, depending on your plan. Your own uploads, narration, music and captions don't use credits.

How much you get

The more seconds you generate, the more credits it uses. Clips are short, so plan in seconds, not minutes.

Read about safety & consent ↗
Do I need editing experience?

No. Describe the result you want or choose a template, and EgaClip handles the production steps.

Can I try it before paying?

You can explore templates, controls and cost estimates before signing in. Sign in to save a project or render.

Does a long plan allowance mean long AI videos?

No. Credits only cover new AI-generated footage. Your finished video can also combine your own media, narration, music and captions.

How do you handle real people and voices?

Use only media, faces and voices you have permission to use. Consent and disclosure controls apply to the relevant workflows.
